# Product Name Candidates — Yeşilvadi Municipal AI Assistant

Three candidate names for the bilingual municipal AI assistant. The final choice will replace the `[PRODUCT NAME]` placeholder across all project documents.

---

## 1. Komşu

| Aspect | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Meaning** | Turkish for "neighbor." Conveys friendliness, proximity, and community — the assistant is like a helpful neighbor who knows how things work at the municipality. |
| **TR pronunciation** | Native Turkish word; intuitive for all Turkish speakers. |
| **EN pronunciation** | "Kom-shoo" — short and phonetically manageable for English speakers, though the "ş" may be simplified to "sh." |
| **Pros** | Warm and approachable; culturally resonant in Turkish neighborhoods; easy to remember; works as a brand identity ("Ask Komşu"). |
| **Cons** | Generic word — harder to trademark. Not yet checked against trademark databases; the word is common in Turkish business names (restaurants, markets). |
| **Brand conflict risk** | Not yet checked; likely crowded in general Turkish commerce. |

---

## 2. Hızla

| Aspect | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Meaning** | Wordplay: "Hızla" means "quickly / with speed" in Turkish; the capitalized "LA" hints at "Language Assistant." Full reading: "serve quickly with a language assistant." |
| **TR pronunciation** | "Hız-la" — natural Turkish, immediately understood as "quickly." |
| **EN pronunciation** | "Hiz-lah" — two syllables, easy to say; the "LA" suffix reads naturally in English. |
| **Pros** | Communicates speed and efficiency; the dual meaning (Turkish speed + English acronym) works for a bilingual product; short and brandable; clean domain potential. |
| **Cons** | The wordplay may not be immediately obvious to non-Turkish speakers. Risk of being perceived as too playful for a government-facing product. |
| **Brand conflict risk** | Not yet checked (trademark search pending). |

---

## 3. Kapım

| Aspect | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Meaning** | Turkish for "my door" (from "kapı" = door). Metaphor: the municipality's door is always open for the citizen. Also echoes "kapı kapı dolaşmak" (going door to door) — the assistant saves you from that. |
| **TR pronunciation** | "Kah-puhm" — natural, two syllables. |
| **EN pronunciation** | "Kah-pim" — short and pronounceable, though the meaning is lost without explanation. |
| **Pros** | Strong metaphor (open door policy, accessibility); personal possessive suffix "-ım" makes it feel citizen-owned; memorable. |
| **Cons** | Meaning is opaque to English-only users. "Kapı" could be confused with unrelated words in other languages. Slightly harder to build an international brand around. |
| **Brand conflict risk** | Not yet checked (trademark search pending). |

---

> **Note:** The final name will be selected by the team in Week 1, as the handbook requires. Until then, all documents use the placeholder `[PRODUCT NAME]`.
